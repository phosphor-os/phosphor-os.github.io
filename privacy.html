Privacy Policy

For Phosphor OS, Phosphor for Windows, Phosphor OS for Android and this website · Effective October 27, 2026

The short version. We will never sell, rent or trade your data. Phosphor has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and collects no personal data from your phone, PC or SuperStation. Your private settings stay on your own devices. Phosphor only goes to the internet for features you use, like scraping box art.

Who we are

Phosphor OS, Phosphor for Windows and Phosphor OS for Android ("Phosphor") and this website are made by f3bandit, an independent individual developer who sells Phosphor OS for Android under the name F3 Digital Systems. Contact details are at the end.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, trade or share your personal data.
  • Phosphor has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or accounts with us.
  • The apps don't read your contacts, photos, messages, location, phone number, accounts, other apps, or advertising and device IDs.
  • We don't have servers that receive data from Phosphor, and there's no cloud relay or tunnel for remote access.

What stays on your devices

Private settings, such as WiFi and Bluetooth pairings, your scraper logins and keys, pairing details, your game library and box art, are stored only on your SuperStation, in the app on your phone or PC, or on your own NVMe drive or NAS. They are never sent over the internet to us. Uninstalling an app or deleting its folder removes what it stored.

What goes over the network, and where

  • Your SuperStation One, over your home network: the apps pair with Phosphor OS using a PIN code shown on your TV, then talk to it directly to show its status, manage your library and make the changes you ask for.
  • ScreenScraper (screenscraper.fr): only when you scrape box art, Phosphor sends the game's details, your own ScreenScraper username and password, and Phosphor's developer ID to ScreenScraper to find the art.
  • Other art sources you choose to use, such as TheGamesDB, IGDB, LaunchBox, the libretro thumbnails and ArcadeItalia: Phosphor sends the game's name or details, plus any API key you entered for that source.
  • GitHub (operated by GitHub, Inc.): Phosphor checks for its own updates, downloads Update All and other MiSTer files you choose, and downloads the fan art list and pack. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website.
  • Google Play: Google handles the purchase, installs and updates of Phosphor OS for Android under Google's privacy policy. The app doesn't contact our website or any other store for license or update checks; Google Play's own services on your device may, as they do for every Play app.

Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Reports you choose to share

Diagnostic reports are created on your own devices and are only shared if you share them yourself. If you post one on GitHub or Discord, it becomes public there. Please read reports before sharing them.

Buying Phosphor OS for Android

Phosphor OS for Android is sold on Google Play. Google processes your purchase under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Google shares with developers (such as the order number, country and price), and use them only for your order, refunds and support.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Phosphor isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because Phosphor keeps data only on your own devices, you can delete it yourself. For order data we hold, contact us and we'll help. You can also contact Google about data Google Play holds.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what Phosphor sends or where, we'll say so in the release notes.
